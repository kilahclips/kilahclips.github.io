Stream highlights, made vertical

Kilah Clips turns the best moments from gaming streams into short vertical videos with captions, published on YouTube Shorts and TikTok. Clips are made for authorized clipping campaigns and always credit the streamer.

YouTube TikTok

Kilah Clipping (publishing tool)

Kilah Clipping is the private tool behind the channel. It finds highlight moments in stream recordings, reframes them to 9:16 with burned-in captions, and, after the owner reviews and approves each clip, uploads it to the owner's own YouTube and TikTok accounts through their official APIs. It has no other users.

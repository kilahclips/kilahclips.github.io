Terms of Service

Use of the tool

  • The tool only publishes to accounts that the owner has connected and authorized through the official YouTube Data API and TikTok Content Posting API.
  • Every clip is reviewed and approved by the owner before it is uploaded. For each TikTok post the owner chooses the privacy level and interaction settings.
  • Clips are made only from content the owner is authorized to use, credit the original streamer, and follow the rules of each clipping campaign and the community guidelines of each platform.
